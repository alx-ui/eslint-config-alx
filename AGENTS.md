## Biome

Use Biome as the formatter, linter and code-quality tool.

Documentation:
https://biomejs.dev/

Configuration:
https://biomejs.dev/guides/configure-biome/

Formatter:
https://biomejs.dev/formatter/

Linter:
https://biomejs.dev/linter/

Always prefer the project's biome.json/biome.jsonc configuration.
Do not introduce ESLint or Prettier configuration unless explicitly requested.
